# 🎼 Symphony Quickstart

Get Symphony on your device, then start (or join) your family — the whole thing takes about a minute.

---

## Step 1: Install Symphony

Pick your device below.

### 🪟 Windows

1. **[Download Symphony-Setup.exe](https://github.com/Amnibro/Symphony/releases/latest/download/Symphony-Setup.exe)** — it's tiny, about 1 MB.
2. Open the downloaded file.
3. You'll probably see a blue screen that says **"Windows protected your PC."** Don't worry — this is completely normal. It just means Symphony comes from a small independent maker instead of a big corporation that pays for a signing certificate. Nothing is wrong with your computer or the app.
4. Click **More info** (small text on that blue screen), then click **Run anyway**.
5. Done! Symphony opens in its own window, like any other app.

> Prefer not to install anything? Download the portable **`Symphony.exe`** from the [releases page](https://github.com/Amnibro/Symphony/releases/latest) instead — just double-click it to run (same "More info → Run anyway" the first time).

### 🤖 Android

1. On your phone, tap this link: **[Download the Android app](https://amni-scient.com/symphony/symphony.apk)**. If your browser warns about the file type, choose **Download anyway**.
2. When it finishes, tap **Open** on the download.
3. Android will say it can't install apps from this browser yet. Tap **Settings**, switch on **Allow from this source**, then go back.
4. Tap **Install**. That's it — Symphony is on your phone, and it keeps itself up to date from now on. No app store needed.

Works on Android 8.0 and newer.

### 🍎 iPhone or iPad

1. Open Safari and go to **[symphony.amni-scient.com](https://symphony.amni-scient.com)**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with the arrow).
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. Symphony now sits on your home screen and opens full-screen, just like an app.

### 💻 Any computer

Just visit **[symphony.amni-scient.com](https://symphony.amni-scient.com)** in any browser. Nothing to install.

## Step 2a: Start your family (60 seconds)

You're the one bringing everyone together? First, one honest note: **the hosted web app at symphony.amni-scient.com is invite-only** — it runs on the developer's own family server, so you can join there (with an invite) or try the demo, but new families start on **your own server**. Good news: that takes about five minutes — download **[symphony-server-win64.zip](https://github.com/Amnibro/Symphony/releases/latest/download/symphony-server-win64.zip)** and follow [SERVER.md](SERVER.md). Once it's running (or on the Android app pointed at your server):

1. **Open Symphony** and type **your name**. That's the entire sign-up — no email, no phone number, no password.
2. Tap **🏠 Start a family** (on the Android app it's **Start My Family**).
3. **Name your family** — "The Smiths," "Camp Grandma," whatever feels right.
4. **Invite your people:** open your family, tap its name at the top, and tap **Invite**. You can **Share link** to send it by text or email, or show the **QR code** for someone standing next to you to scan.

Send that invite to everyone — grandparents, cousins, the group chat. Whoever opens it joins your family.

> **One small thing:** keep your app open for a bit after people join, so it can wave hello to each new member and finish the encrypted handshake. After that, everyone sees everyone.

## Step 2b: Join your family (60 seconds)

Someone sent you an invite? Even easier:

1. **Tap the invite link** they sent you (or, on Android, open Symphony → **Join My Family** → **Scan a QR code** and point your camera at their code). On a computer, you can also paste the link into the box on the welcome screen.
2. Type **your name**.
3. Tap **Join**. You're in — say hi!

---

## What does an invite link look like?

It's a long jumble of letters and symbols that starts like this:

```
symphony://space?…
```

That jumble isn't random decoration — it **is** the encryption. The link carries your family's sealed key, so only people you give it to can join. You can share it any way you like: text message, email, a QR code, written on a birthday card — anything. Just remember it's the key to your family's private space, so share it like one.
