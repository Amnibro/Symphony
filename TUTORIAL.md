## Your identity & name

The first time you open Symphony, it asks for one thing: **your name**. Type it, and you exist. No email, no phone number, no password, no account.

Behind the scenes, your device quietly creates a **private key** — a unique, unguessable identity that lives only on your device. That key is what proves you're you, and it's what locks and unlocks your messages.

A few things worth knowing:

- **Keep using the same device or browser** to stay "you." Your identity lives there.
- **There's no password to forget.** There's also no password to reset — the key on your device *is* the whole thing.
- **Each device is its own person.** If you open Symphony on your phone *and* your laptop, each one creates its own identity, so you'll appear as two separate members. For now, pick one device per "you."
- On the web welcome screen there's a **🔑 I have a recovery code** option for restoring an existing identity if you saved a recovery code.

## Starting a family

A **Family** is your private space — invite-only, end-to-end encrypted, invisible to everyone you haven't invited.

1. Open Symphony and type your name.
2. Tap **🏠 Start a family** (on the Android app it's **Start My Family**).
3. Give it a name — "The Smiths," "Sunday Dinner Crew," anything.

That's it. You can start as many families as you like — one for the whole family, one for the siblings, one for the book club. They each get their own space, and Symphony's home screen lists them all.

Inside a family you'll find two places to talk:

- **# General** — the back-and-forth group chat.
- **# Feed** — where posts live: photos, updates, events, polls. More like a bulletin board than a chat.

## Inviting people

Open your family, tap its **name at the top**, then tap **Invite**. You get two ways to bring someone in:

- **📱 QR code (Android app):** a code appears on your screen. Whoever's joining just points their phone camera at it. Perfect when you're in the same room — this is the famous "grandkid sets up Grandma" move.
- **🔗 Share link:** tap **Share link** and send it by text, email, or anything else. The link starts with `symphony://space?…` followed by a long jumble — that jumble is your family's sealed, encrypted key, so **only people you give it to can join**.

Share the link however you like. It works from any device — phone, tablet, or computer.

> **After someone joins:** keep your app open for a little while. Your app and the newcomer's app do a quick encrypted handshake to exchange keys, and your app needs to be awake for that to happen. Once it does, you're connected for good.

## Joining a family

Someone sent you an invite? You have three ways in, all under a minute:

- **Tap the link** they sent you. Symphony opens and asks for your name — type it, tap **Join**, done.
- **Scan the QR code** (Android app): open Symphony → **Join My Family** → **Scan a QR code** → point your camera at their screen.
- **Paste the link** (web): on the welcome screen at [symphony.amni-scient.com](https://symphony.amni-scient.com), paste the invite into the box and tap **Join family**.

If you're already in Symphony, you can join more families any time from the home screen's **＋** button.

## Chatting

The chat does everything you'd hope, in every family:

- **📷 Photos** — send from your gallery or camera. Photos open in a full-screen viewer with a download button.
- **🎤 Voice notes** — hold, talk, send. Great for the family members who'd rather talk than type.
- **📎 Files** — send documents, PDFs, whatever needs sharing.
- **🎞️ GIFs and stickers** — because some feelings need a dancing cat.
- **❤️ Reactions** — tap a message and pick an emoji. Double-tap for a quick heart.
- **↩️ Replies** — reply to a specific message so nobody loses the thread. On a touchscreen, just swipe a message to reply to it.
- **@Mentions** — type @ and a name to get someone's attention.
- **📌 Pins** — pin the important stuff (the address, the plan, the recipe) so it stays at the top.
- **⭐ Saved messages** — star anything to keep it, then view your saved list any time.
- **🔍 Search** — find that thing someone said three weeks ago.
- **Edit, delete, copy, forward** — tap and hold a message for its menu. You can edit or delete your own messages.
- **Drafts and badges** — half-typed messages are saved per conversation, and unread chats show a badge.

Links you paste show a preview, and the big moments get a little extra — confetti and heart bursts when they're deserved.

## The Feed

The **# Feed** is your family's own timeline — photos, updates, announcements — with a crucial difference from every other social app: **there is no algorithm**. Posts appear because your people posted them, newest first. No ads, no "suggested content," no strangers.

- **Post** photos and updates for the whole family to see.
- **Like** posts and leave **comments** — the grandparents' favorite feature.
- Events and polls show up here too, so nobody misses the birthday plan.

Some looks (Facebook, Instagram, Midnight) open straight into the Feed; others (Classic, Discord, Messenger) open into Chats. Either way, both are always one tap apart.

## Stories

Share a moment that doesn't need to live forever. **✨ Stories** are photos your family can watch for **24 hours** — then they quietly disappear. Perfect for the everyday little things: the dog being ridiculous, the first snow, tonight's dinner.

---

## Polls & events

- **📊 Polls** — ask a question, add choices, let everyone vote. "Pizza or tacos Saturday?" settled in minutes, democratically.
- **📅 Events** — post the birthday party, the reunion, the game — with the details in one place so nobody has to scroll for them.

Both live right in your family, encrypted like everything else.

## Video calls

Inside a family, tap **📞** at the top to start a call. Calls are encrypted and connect device-to-device, with:

- **Voice and video**, one-on-one or the whole group at once.
- **Screen sharing** — show Grandma the photos, or let the family tech support see your screen.

**Honest note:** calls are on the **Android app only** right now. The web version (computers, iPhone, iPad) can chat, post, and do everything else — but it can't join calls yet. Calls also aren't offered in the public Community; they're just for the people you've invited.

## Themes & looks

Symphony can wear a whole different outfit. Your families, messages and photos stay exactly the same — only the look changes.

- **🎼 Classic** — Symphony's cozy, calm default.
- **📘 Facebook** — clean blue-on-grey; opens on your Feed.
- **🎮 Discord** — dark and chat-first, with a compact message list.
- **📸 Instagram** — bright and photo-first; opens on your Feed.
- **💬 Messenger** — bright blue with big rounded chat bubbles.
- **🌙 Midnight** — a true-black timeline, easy on the eyes.

**Where to change it:** a chooser appears the first time you open Symphony. Later, it's **Settings → Choose your look** on the phone, or the **🎨** button (or Settings) on the web. On top of any look you can also pick an **accent color** — blue, green, purple and more — in the same Appearance picker.

If a family member says "I liked it the old way," they can have it the old way. Everyone picks their own look; it never changes anyone else's.

## Your profile

Tap anyone's **name or avatar** to see their quick profile — who they are and what they've been posting. Set your own name and photo from your profile (the **You** tab on the web) so everyone recognizes you.

Remember: there are no usernames or accounts underneath. A profile is just the friendly face on top of that device's unique encrypted identity.

## Notifications

- **Android:** Symphony notifies you about new messages while the app is running. Notifications when the app is *fully closed* are still in the works — so if things seem quiet, pop the app open to catch up.
- **Web / iPhone / computer:** you'll see new messages and unread badges whenever Symphony is open.

A good habit for now: check in like you'd check the mailbox. Your messages always wait for you — the relay holds sealed envelopes until your app picks them up.

## The public Community

Beyond your private families, Symphony has one big shared space: the **🌐 Community**. Think of it as the town square — everyone on Symphony is automatically a member, no invite needed.

- **Open it:** tap the **🌐** globe in the top bar (phone) or the **🌐 Community** button (web). A dot on the globe means there are new posts, and a **↓** button inside jumps you to the latest.
- **Post and share photos**, **react** (👍 ❤️ 😂 😮 😢 🔥), and **reply** — replies quote the post they answer, and tapping the quote jumps you back.
- **See who's who:** tap a name or avatar for their quick profile.
- **Keep it comfortable:** tap a post → **🔇 Mute this person** to hide everything from them. Unmute any time. Muting is private to you.
- **Photos here are kept small** so they reach everyone reliably — use a family for full-resolution sharing.

### 🧩 Sub-communities (topic groups)

Don't want the whole square at once? Join topic groups inside the Community:

- Tap **＋ topic** (web) or **🧩 Join or create a sub-community** (phone) and type a topic — `gardening`, `recipes`, `austin-tx`.
- Anyone who types the **same topic name** lands in the **same encrypted space**. There's no central list; the name itself is the key. Suggested starters (Gardening, Recipes, Tech, Sports, Music, Books, Pets, Travel, Faith, Parenting) appear when you explore.
- **Switch** between Main and your topics with the chips at the top, and **share a topic** with its own link — whoever opens it lands in that exact group.

**One important difference:** families are private and invite-only. The Community is **public** — anyone with Symphony can read and post there. It's still encrypted in transit (the relays can't read it), but treat it like a public square, because it is one.

## Privacy FAQ

**What actually leaves my device?**
Only sealed, encrypted envelopes. Every message, photo, and call is encrypted *on your device* before it goes anywhere, using keys that only your family members hold.

**What does the server see?**
Symphony uses a relay — a store-and-forward mail carrier that holds envelopes until the recipient's app picks them up. The relay sees that envelopes are moving, but **only ever sees ciphertext** — scrambled data it cannot read. No plaintext, ever.

**What's the actual encryption?**
Your identity is an Ed25519 + X25519 key pair generated on your device. Messages are sealed with X25519 key agreement → HKDF-SHA256 → ChaCha20-Poly1305.

**Are there ads or tracking?**
No. No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no data collection, no phone number, no email. There's no account to build a profile on — that's the point.

**Is the Community as private as my family?**
Family messages can only be read by the members you invited. Community posts are encrypted to a shared key that every Symphony app has — so relays still can't read them, but anyone *with the app* can. Private things belong in your family, not the square.

**What control do I have?**
Mute anyone in the Community, leave any family at any time, and clear a chat from your device. Your invite links only work for people you give them to.

## Troubleshooting

**Windows shows "Windows protected your PC."**
Normal! It appears because Symphony is from a small independent maker, not because anything is wrong. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. You'll only see it the first time.

**"My family joined but they can't see me yet" (or I joined and can't see them).**
The person who sent the invite needs to have their app **open once** after you join — the two apps do a quick encrypted handshake to exchange keys. Ask them to open Symphony for a minute, and you'll pop right in. After that first handshake, you're connected for good.

**"I tapped the invite link and got a blank page."**
Your app or page is a step behind. On Android, open Symphony and let it update itself; on the web, reload the page. Then tap the invite link again.

**"The invite link doesn't open the app."**
Install Symphony first, then tap the link again.

**"I don't see the Community."**
Tap the **🌐** globe — top bar on the phone, header button on the web. You're already a member.

**"Android won't let me install the app."**
That's Android being careful about apps from outside the Play Store. When it asks, tap **Settings → Allow from this source**, go back, and tap **Install**. Full steps are in the [Quickstart](QUICKSTART.md).

**"I show up as two different people."**
Each device creates its own identity, so your phone-you and your laptop-you are separate members. Stick with one device per person for now.
